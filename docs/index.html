Social Poster

Social media management in one place

Manage your social content more easily

Social Poster is a web application designed to help users connect authorized social media accounts and manage photo publishing workflows from one place.

How it works

  1. Connect an eligible social media account through its official authorization process.
  2. Select the supported account or page you manage.
  3. Choose a photo and use the publishing features available for your connected account.

Features depend on each platform's permissions, eligibility, and API restrictions.

Account security and privacy

Only connect accounts you are authorized to manage. Review our policies to understand the terms of use and how personal information is handled.
